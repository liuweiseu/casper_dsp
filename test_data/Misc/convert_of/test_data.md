# convert_of test data

`convert_of` is `convert` (signed) plus casper_library's overflow flag: `of` = 1 when the top `wb_lost+1` bits of `din` are not all equal. The first cycles walk corner words (0, 1, all-ones, MSB only, MSB−1, MSB+1); the rest are seeded random.

Generated by `test_data/scripts/gen_butterfly_test_data.py` (Python reference model, not exported from MATLAB). Files: `sim_din.csv`, expected `sim_out.csv` (dout) and `sim_of.csv`, one raw unsigned value per cycle.

| Test # | Directory | N_BITS_IN | BIN_PT_IN | N_BITS_OUT | BIN_PT_OUT | QUANTIZATION | OVERFLOW | LATENCY | Cycles | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `simdata0` | 16 | 8 | 8 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 256 | Fix_16_8 → Fix_8_4, truncate + wrap, combinational |
| 1 | `simdata1` | 16 | 12 | 8 | 4 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 256 | Fix_16_12 → Fix_8_4, round + saturate |
| 2 | `simdata2` | 12 | 10 | 12 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 256 | same integer bits: overflow impossible, of = 0 |
| 3 | `simdata3` | 18 | 16 | 18 | 17 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 256 | one integer bit dropped (butterfly-style), round even |
| 4 | `simdata4` | 8 | 4 | 12 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 256 | bit growth: of = 0 |
