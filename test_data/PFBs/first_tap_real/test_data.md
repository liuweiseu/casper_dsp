# first_tap_real test data

`first_tap_real` is tap_real with DELAY = 2^(PFB_SIZE−N_INPUTS)·N_POL_BLOCKS and the full TOTAL_TAPS-coefficient bus of pfb_coeff_gen (it uses the lowest slice, ROM TOTAL_TAPS).

Generated by `test_data/scripts/gen_pfb_test_data.py` (reference model of the block's internal diagram in casper_library_pfbs.slx, not exported from MATLAB). Data and coefficient words are random, with corner words (most negative × most negative, …) at the start; `sync` pulses periodically plus one early re-sync. CSV rows are cycles; values are raw words.

**CSV data exported from the corresponding MATLAB Simulink block.**

Simulink data: the `sim_*.csv` files of sets 0–2 were exported from Simulink models of the block (MATLAB R2021a, System Generator; plugins-demo2 export flow with its own random stimulus) and copied here on 2026-10-14; `params.json` (and any `.mem` files) are the generator's. Regenerating the module with its script replaces the exported CSVs with the reference model's output.
