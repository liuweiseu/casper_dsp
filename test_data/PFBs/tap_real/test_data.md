# tap_real test data

`tap_real` multiplies its own input sample by the lowest COEFF_WIDTH bits of the coefficient bus, forwards the rest of the bus without delay and delays data and sync by DELAY. Chain check: a whole first_tap_real → tap_real → last_tap_real chain was compared with the windowed-presum PFB definition on integer data: 2432 outputs in 5 configurations (PFBSize 4–6, 2–8 taps, n_inputs 0–2, n_pol_blocks 1–3) matched exactly, and per-hop delays of delay−1, delay+1 and 0 were all detected as wrong.

Generated by `test_data/scripts/gen_pfb_test_data.py` (reference model of the block's internal diagram in casper_library_pfbs.slx, not exported from MATLAB). Data and coefficient words are random, with corner words (most negative × most negative, …) at the start; `sync` pulses periodically plus one early re-sync. CSV rows are cycles; values are raw words.
